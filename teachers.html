TELEGRAM MCQ · FOR CLASS TEACHERS

Connect your class.

Use Telegram on your phone and your class link from the coordinator.

  1. 01

    Create a class group

    Or use an existing group where you can add the bot.

  2. 02

    Tap your class link

    Open the complete link on your phone.

  3. 03

    Select the group & add the bot

    Allow it to send messages, polls and images.

  4. 04

    Check “Connected successfully”

    Look in the group, then tell your coordinator you’re ready.

Link not working?

Send the full link to Telegram Saved Messages and tap it on your phone. Still stuck? Contact your coordinator.
